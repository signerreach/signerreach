# Code of conduct

SignerReach is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in it, in
issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to follow this.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with an idea, not with a person.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. Explain what is wrong and, when you can,
  how to fix it.
- Say so when you do not know. Do not guess and present it as fact. This applies
  to the tool and to how we talk about it.
- Welcome newcomers. A first contribution deserves a patient, clear review.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Personal attacks, threats, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or spam.
- Pressure on maintainers for faster reviews or for decisions.

## Reporting

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting: open the **Security** tab of
this repository, choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with
"Conduct report". This reaches the maintainers privately. For something in a
public comment, you can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on it.

Reports are handled by the maintainers on a best-effort basis. The person who
reported is not named to others without their agreement.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests,
and block people from the project when conduct is not acceptable. This applies
to anyone, including maintainers and members of the team.
